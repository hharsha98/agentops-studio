# Support Policy Playbook

## Refund window

Customers may request a full refund within **14 days** of purchase when the
service was unavailable for more than 4 continuous hours. Outside that window,
offer account credit instead of a cash refund unless Legal approves an exception.

## Escalation

Escalate to a human when:
- the customer mentions legal action
- PII may have been exposed
- the refund amount exceeds $500

## Tone

Be concise, empathetic, and cite the policy section you relied on.
Never invent policy. Prefer draft-only Gmail replies until approval.
